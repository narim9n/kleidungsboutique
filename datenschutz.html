Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Protecting your personal data matters to me. This policy explains which data is processed when you visit this website and place an order, why that happens, and what rights you have.

1. Controller

Nariman Ghanai, 65936 Frankfurt am Main, Germany
Email: nghanai@icloud.com

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is delivered through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your browser transmits technical data that is processed temporarily: IP address, date and time of the request, the requested page, the browser type and the operating system. This is necessary to display the site, keep it stable and secure it against abuse.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and working website). Cloudflare may process data in the USA. The transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

3. Placing an order

When you place an order, I process the data you enter: your first and last name, email address, optional phone number, delivery address, an optional message, and the items and total of your order. I use it to confirm your order, communicate with you, ship the item and handle payment.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performing a contract) and, for tax and accounting duties, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. I delete the data once it is no longer needed. Records I am legally required to keep, such as invoices and payment records, are stored for the statutory retention period of up to ten years.

You are not required to give me your data, but without it I cannot process an order.

4. Form service (Formsubmit)

Orders are sent to me through the form service Formsubmit (formsubmit.co). Formsubmit forwards the data you enter to my email inbox and acts as a technical service provider in doing so. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. I use the service to receive orders without running my own server.

5. Order confirmation email

After you place an order, I send an automatic confirmation to the email address you entered. For this I use the email service EmailJS (emailjs.com). The service receives your name, email address, delivery address and the details of your order so that it can create and send the message. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performing a contract).

6. Address suggestions

In the checkout, the address search field suggests addresses while you type and fills in the address fields for you. For this, the text you type into that field is sent to the geocoding service Photon (photon.komoot.io), which is operated by komoot GmbH, Potsdam, Germany, and uses OpenStreetMap data. Your IP address is transmitted to the service as well. Nothing is sent until you have typed at least four characters, and you can skip the search and enter your address by hand.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a convenient checkout).

7. Notification about unfinished orders

If you add items to your cart and leave the website without ordering, I receive an automatic email. It contains only the items in the cart and the total. It does not contain your name, email address or any other personal data, and it is not linked to you as a person. I use it to understand how the shop is used and to improve it.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the shop).

8. Cart storage in your browser

Your cart is saved locally in your browser (local storage) so it is still there when you come back. This information stays on your device and is not sent to me until you place an order. It is strictly necessary to provide the shopping function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data.

9. Payment with PayPal

After you place an order, you can pay through PayPal (PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A., 22–24 Boulevard Royal, L-2449 Luxembourg). When you click the PayPal button, you leave this website and enter PayPal's own pages. From that point PayPal is responsible for processing your payment data, and its privacy policy applies. I receive the payment information that PayPal provides to me, such as the amount and the sender's name.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

10. Fonts

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). When you open a page, your browser connects to Google's servers, which transmits your IP address to Google. I use this for a consistent and attractive presentation of the site.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in an attractive presentation).

11. Links to social media

The links to Instagram and TikTok are plain links. No data is sent to these platforms by visiting this website. Only after you click a link do you reach the respective platform, whose own privacy policy then applies.

12. Contacting me

If you write to me by email or through Instagram, I process your message and the contact details you provide in order to answer your request. I delete it once your request is dealt with, unless I am legally required to keep it.

13. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data I hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • have the processing restricted (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing that is based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • withdraw any consent you gave, with effect for the future

To use any of these rights, just email me at the address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for me is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (HBDI) in Wiesbaden.

14. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the website or the legal situation changes. The current version is always the one published on this page.
