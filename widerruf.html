Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, I will reimburse all payments I have received from you, including the costs of delivery (except for additional costs resulting from your choice of a type of delivery other than the least expensive standard delivery I offer). I will do so without undue delay and no later than 14 days from the day on which I receive your notice of withdrawal. I will use the same means of payment that you used for the original transaction, unless we expressly agree otherwise. You will not be charged any fees for this reimbursement.

I may withhold the reimbursement until I have received the goods back or you have provided proof that you have sent them back, whichever is earlier.

Please send the goods back or hand them over to me without undue delay and no later than 14 days from the day on which you notify me of your withdrawal. The deadline is met if you send the goods before the 14 days have expired. You bear the direct cost of returning the goods.

You are only liable for any loss in value of the goods if it results from handling beyond what is necessary to check the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods. Please return the piece unworn, unwashed and in the condition you received it, so that it can be checked.
